# An Interactive Cloud-Based Dashboard for a Retail Manager
Analyzed GoOutside’s retailer network using SQL in BigQuery and developed an interactive Looker Studio dashboard to evaluate market structure, retailer performance, and market share.

## Project overview

As part of my WBS Data Analytics course, I took on the role of a Data Analyst to develop analytical tools for the Head of Retail Partnership at GoOutside, a growing supplier of adventure and camping equipment.
This was my first project involving data visualization with Looker Studio, giving me the opportunity to apply SQL-based data analysis and transform the results into an interactive business dashboard.
Within a five-day project timeframe, i was tasked with developing a tool that would provide the Head of Retail Partnership with actionable insights into the performance and structure of GoOutside's retailer network.

## Business Requirements

The Head of Retail Partnership wanted detailed information about the performance and structure of GoOutside's retail markets.
The main objectives were to:

- Analyze the composition of different markets.
- Determine whether a market is competitive or dominant based on the concentration of sales among the largest retailers.
- Monitor changes in sales volume over the selected period.
- Monitor changes in the number of active retailers.
  
Based on these requirements, i had five days to develop a useful analytical tool that would provide the required information and support strategic decision-making.

## Methodology

The data of interest was extracted and transformed using SQL queries in BigQuery and subsequently connected to Looker Studio.
The resulting analysis was implemented as a two-page interactive dashboard, allowing users to filter the results by region and time period.

## Dashboard 1 - Retail Market Strategy


The first dashboard page provides two main filters:
- Region – geographical regions were defined using SQL.
- Date range – allows users to select the period of interest.
Based on the selected filters, the dashboard provides the following information.

### Market Strategy
The dashboard shows the proportion of total sales generated by the three largest retailers.
Based on this concentration, the market is classified according to the project's definition as either:

- Dominant Market – the three largest retailers account for at least 60% of total sales.
- Competitive Market – the three largest retailers account for less than 60% of total sales.
  
This classification provides the basis for the respective market growth strategy.

### Market Development

The dashboard displays two key indicators for the selected period:

- Sales Volume Growth
- Growth or decline in the number of active retailers
  
These indicators provide insight into how the selected market has developed over time and whether the respective strategic objectives are being achieved.

### Top Retailers
At the bottom of the page, a bar chart displays the five most important retailers based on sales volume.
This allows users to quickly identify the retailers contributing most to GoOutside's sales.


## Dashboard 2 - Market Composition

The second dashboard page provides the same two filters:

- Region
- Date range
  
An additional Market Share parameter is included. Currently, this parameter uses Sales Volume as its metric.
The parameter was designed to allow additional metrics to be incorporated in the future if the Head of Retail Partnership requires further perspectives on market composition.

### Active Retailers

Below the filters, an information box displays the number of active retailers corresponding to the selected filter criteria.

### Retailer Market Share

The pie chart shows the distribution of total sales volume among the retailers in the selected market.
This provides a quick overview of GoOutside's most important retail partners and can support decisions regarding the future of individual partnerships, particularly when evaluating poorly performing retailers.

### Retailers by Country

The bar chart displays the number of retailers in each country within the selected region.
The chart is interactive: when a user selects a country, the pie chart responds accordingly and displays the sales volume distribution among retailers within that national market.
This allows users to move from an overview of a broader regional market to the composition of an individual national market.

## Future Work

The short five-day timeframe left considerable room for further development.

### Dashboard Design

The dashboards could be made more visually polished and user-friendly. Due to the limited project timeframe, the primary focus was on implementing the required analytical functionality.

### Additional KPIs

As mentioned above, the Market Share parameter could be expanded to include additional KPIs, such as:

- Profit
- Profit margin
- Number of products sold
- Other relevant performance indicators
- Average Order Sales Volume
- Average Order Cost

This would allow the Head of Retail Partnership to evaluate market composition from multiple perspectives rather than relying solely on sales volume.

### Additional Tools

With a longer development period, google sheets could have been implemented to offer a deeper insight into the data.

Google sheets were planned to offer detailed tables showing the overall productivity of each and every retailer of the company to offer the retail manager a deeper insight into each retailer.
